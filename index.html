Frequently asked questions

How is "cost per egg" calculated?

Total expenses in the selected period divided by total eggs collected in that period. The Insights tab also shows a net figure that subtracts your egg-sale income — if it goes negative, congratulations, your flock is profitable.

Do I have to assign eggs to individual hens?

No — the daily total is all that's required. Per-hen attribution is optional: open a day in the Egg log and tally who laid. It powers the per-hen stats and the "top layer" insight.

How does molt tracking work?

Open a hen's profile and tap Start molt when the feathers start flying, and Mark molt finished when she's back in business. The app keeps a history of every molt with its duration.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings → Export everything as CSV produces four spreadsheet-ready files (eggs, hens, expenses, sales) via the standard iOS share sheet.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your data lives only on your device — the app has no servers and no accounts (see our privacy policy). A device backup (iCloud or computer) includes the app's data like any other app. CSV export is a good periodic safety net too.

What's the sample data for?

It's a demo flock so you can explore the app with realistic numbers. Remove it anytime with Settings → Remove sample data — anything you added yourself is kept.
